Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What's collected:

  • Account information you provide when registering: email address and username.
  • Reading activity if you're logged in: bookmarks, reading progress, likes, comments, and reviews you choose to post.
  • Basic, anonymous usage data (such as which pages are viewed) used only to understand what's being read and improve the site.
  • Payment details if you choose to donate. Card and payment information is handled entirely by Razorpay — this site never receives or stores your card details directly.

How it's used: to run your account, remember your reading progress and preferences, display your comments/reviews with your username, and process donations you choose to make. Data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Your choices: you can update your profile information at any time while logged in. To request that your account or personal data be deleted, use the contact details below — this is currently handled manually rather than through a self-service option.

This site uses your browser's local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to remember simple preferences like your theme and font size choice.

Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

My Novel World does not sell or ship any physical products. All content on this site — novels, chapters, and updates — is digital and available to read directly on the site at no cost. Cancellation & Refunds

Last updated: August 2026

Reading on My Novel World is free and does not require payment, so there is nothing to cancel for access to content.

Donations made through this site are voluntary and, as a general rule, non-refundable, since they don't purchase a product or service. That said, if you're charged in error — for example, a duplicate charge, an incorrect amount due to a technical fault, or an unauthorized transaction — reach out within 7 days of the charge through the contact details below, and it will be looked into and, where appropriate, refunded to your original payment method through Razorpay.
